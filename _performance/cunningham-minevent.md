  description: |
    Exploring the interfaces between body, motion, gesture and projected image, this work enables dancers to become "brushes" serendipitously creating a painterly composition through performance. The real-time reactive graphics system that I created for this recent re-staging of a Cunningham m*inEvent* utilizes expressive artificial intelligence to engage Cunningham's aesthetic principles of indeterminacy to complement and, at times, amplify the dancers' movements on stage.
